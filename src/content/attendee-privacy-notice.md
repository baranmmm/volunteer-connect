# Event attendee privacy notice

Version 1.0 · 24 November 2026

This notice explains how the Dialogue Society uses your personal information when you register for one of our public events without an account, or when you sign up to receive emails about our events ("Get event updates"). If you have a Volunteer Hub account, our main privacy notice covers your account and the events you register for while signed in.

## Who we are

The Dialogue Society is a registered charity in England and Wales (charity number 1117039), 29 High Holborn, London WC1V 6AZ. The Dialogue Society is the data controller for the information described here.

## What we collect

| Information | When we collect it |
| --- | --- |
| Your name and email address | When you register for an event as a guest |
| Your phone number (optional) | If you give it when you register |
| The number of places you book | When you register |
| Your registration status (waiting for your confirmation, registered, on the waiting list, cancelled) and whether you attended | When you register, confirm, cancel, and when the organisers check people in at the event |
| When you confirmed or cancelled your registration | When you use the links in our emails |
| For event updates: your email address, your name (optional) and the branch you are interested in (optional) | When you sign up for event updates, or tick "Keep me informed about future events" when you register |
| For event updates: when you gave your consent, where you signed up (our website or an event registration), when you confirmed your subscription and when you unsubscribed | When you sign up, confirm or unsubscribe |
| A record of the emails we send you | When we send them |

We do not ask for special category data (such as health, religion or ethnicity). We do not store your IP address. To stop automated abuse of our forms we count requests using a scrambled (hashed) form of it, which cannot be turned back into the address and is deleted within 2 days.

## Why we use it and our lawful basis

- Managing your event registration: confirming your email address, giving you a place or a place on the waiting list, telling you if a place becomes free, sending you the event details, updates (for example a change of time or venue), a reminder before the event and a link to cancel, and checking people in at the event. Our lawful basis is that you asked us to register you (taking steps at your request) and our legitimate interests in running safe and well-organised events. We use your phone number only to contact you about the event you registered for, for example about a last-minute change.
- Event updates (announcements of future events): only if you sign up for them or tick "Keep me informed about future events", and only after you confirm your email address with the link we send you. Our lawful basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every update email; this does not affect emails about events you have registered for.
- Keeping our forms secure: our legitimate interests in preventing spam and automated sign-ups.

## Who can see it

| Who | What they can see |
| --- | --- |
| The organisers of the event (the Branch Managers of the event's branch and the Regional Directors of its region) | The guest list of that event: names, email addresses, phone numbers, places and attendance, to run the event and check people in |
| National Coordinators | Numbers only (how many people registered or attended); never your details |
| Other attendees and the public | Nothing. Guest names are never shown on the public website or to other attendees |

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. We use these service providers, under contracts that protect your data:

- Supabase: database hosting (hosted in the EU)
- Lovable: hosting of the web app
- Brevo: sending our emails
- Cloudflare Turnstile, when it is switched on for our forms: a security check that processes technical information about your browser and device (including your IP address) to tell people from automated programs

## How long we keep it

- Guest registrations are deleted 12 months after the event ends.
- Registrations you do not confirm are deleted after 48 hours.
- Event update subscriptions you do not confirm are deleted after 7 days.
- Records of the emails we sent you are deleted after 30 days.
- If you unsubscribe from event updates, we delete your name and branch interest and keep only your email address with the date you unsubscribed, so that we never email you updates again.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Access the information we hold about you
- Correct information that is wrong
- Ask us to erase your information
- Object to how we use your information or ask us to restrict it
- Withdraw your consent to event updates at any time (use the unsubscribe link in any update email)

You can cancel a registration at any time before the event starts with the link in your confirmation email. To use any of the other rights, email info@dialoguesociety.org. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF, www.ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

## Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version on this page. Each version is numbered and dated.
